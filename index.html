Understanding the Four Tempers

An internal resource for Lumon Staff

Why the Tempers Matter

First created by Kier Eagan, the tempers represent the four core emotional components of the human soul. Each of these four tempers is associated with a specific feeling or archetype.Taming these tempers allows for one to gain mastery over themselves and the world.

The Four Tempers

  • Woe
  • Frolic
  • Dread
  • Malice

Woe

Sadness and melancholy. Represented by a crying woman.

Frolic

Joy and frivolity. Represented by a jester.

Dread

Fear, represented by an old woman with a terrified expression

Malice

Rage and anger, represented by a ram.
