Why the Tempers Matter

First created by Kier Eagan, the tempers represent the four core emotional components of the human soul. Taming these tempers allows for one to gain mastery over themselves and the world.

The Four Tempers

  • Woe
  • Frolic
  • Dread
  • Malice
